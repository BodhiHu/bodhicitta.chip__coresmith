## Explicit non-goals / open backend items
- **gfx1250 binary/ROCr/hipBLASLt compatibility**: not implemented; source/API
  shim only.
- **WMMA flat P&R**: not converged (1,024-bit LDS buses / 2,096 pins).
- **Full WGP synthesis**: WMMA expansion makes ABC exceed the 600 s budget;
  serial divider removed the softmax-specific timeout.
- **FlashAttention RTL**: model only; there is no FP16 RTL matrix path.
- **SFU as a standalone SIMD instruction**: SFU is used by softmax_engine but
  not exposed as a separate instruction.

These limits are documented in `spec/AI_SCOPE.md`, `spec/ROCM_COMPAT.md`,
`doc/OPERATORS.md`, and `doc/BACKEND.md`.
