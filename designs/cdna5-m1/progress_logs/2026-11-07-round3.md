  `/` tokens in the PDK `macro_sparecell` subckt, and dropping the `V ...
  DC 0` jumpers, extraction reached the comparison section but was OOM-killed
  with the 126k-cell WMMA P&R running concurrently.  LVS remains open; DRC is
  0 and route DRC is 0.  The extracted/patched CDL is git-ignored.
- WMMA: 126,245 cells / 1.218 mm².  Two P&R attempts on the 15 GB host:
  * 45% utilization: repair_design inserted the buffers for the 8,196-fanout
    enable net; detailed route reached 40% but stalled at 126,726 violations.
  * 30% utilization: same flow; detailed route completed the 0th iteration at
    90% with 255,357 violations, then the 1st iteration reached 355,997 and
    was stopped.  The 2,096-pin block (1,024-bit LDS read + write buses) is
    not routable in this flat, flop-LDS form.  A routeable build needs the
    LDS/VGPR buses split into narrower interfaces or mapped to SRAM macros,
    or a hierarchical/chiplet partition; the matrix-core RTL itself is
    functionally verified and synthesized.
